# Market Intelligence Skill

Real-time order book depth imbalance, spread analysis, and market overview across Bitget Spot and Mix Futures.

## Tools
- `get_order_book_depth(symbol, limit)`: Measures bid/ask volume depth ratios and liquidity skew.
- `get_market_overview()`: Returns live market telemetry for top crypto and 7x24 tokenized US stocks.
- `get_ticker_price(symbol)`: Fetches current trade price for any asset.
