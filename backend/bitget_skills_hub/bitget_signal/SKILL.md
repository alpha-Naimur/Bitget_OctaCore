# Bitget Signal Skill (Official 5 Research Skills)

Official perception skills package for Bitget OctaCore, providing macroeconomic, sentiment, on-chain, news, and technical analysis without requiring credentials.

## Tools
- `get_macro_analysis()`: Macro & cross-asset correlation (Fed policy, BTC vs Gold/DXY/Nasdaq).
- `get_market_intel()`: On-chain flows, ETF capital tracking, institutional whale telemetry.
- `get_news_briefing()`: Narrative synthesis and breaking headlines.
- `get_sentiment_analysis()`: Fear & Greed Index, long/short ratio, funding rates.
- `get_technical_analysis(symbol, interval)`: Multi-period technical indicator suite.
